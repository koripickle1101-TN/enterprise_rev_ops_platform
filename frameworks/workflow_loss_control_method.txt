Kori Pickle Workflow Loss Control Method

Purpose
This portfolio method helps healthcare operations teams identify where a workflow first lost control before the breakdown becomes authorization delay, payer friction, rework, denial risk, staff burden, or patient access frustration.

Core Question
Where did the workflow first lose control?

Five Control Domains
Patient Access Control
Authorization Readiness Control
Documentation Control
Ownership Control
Time Control

Output Categories
Stable
Watch
Control Loss
Escalation Required

Human Review Boundary
This method does not automate payer decisions, clinical decisions, billing determinations, coding determinations, or patient specific recommendations. It supports operational visibility using synthetic data only.

Portfolio Value
A dashboard reports activity. This method explains where control failed, why the record needs attention, who owns the next action, and what should be stabilized before downstream damage occurs.
